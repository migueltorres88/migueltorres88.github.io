Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how the MiniPOP app for Android (the “app”), made by Miguel Torres, handles information from the people who use it. The app is suitable for everyone, including children under 13, and follows the Google Play Families Policy.

Summary

  • The app does not ask for names, email, phone numbers, photos, location or any other personal data.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-up and no chat with other people.
  • Game progress is stored only on the phone.
  • It shows Google ads set up for children: no personalized ads and only content suitable for everyone.
  • Purchases are charged by Google Play: the app never sees payment details.

1. Information the app stores

The app stores game progress on the phone itself: stars, records, levels, unfinished games, lives, the chosen language and settings such as sound. This information is not sent to any MiniPOP server (there is none).

If the phone has Android backup turned on, the system may back up that progress to the Google account of the phone's owner, so it can be restored on a new device. Google performs and manages that backup under its own policy, and it can be turned off in the phone's settings.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads: an optional ad to get lives back and, from time to time, a full-screen ad between levels. Every ad request is set up like this:

  • tagged as child-directed and for users under the age of consent;
  • no personalized or interest-based ads;
  • maximum content rating G (suitable for all audiences);
  • the app does not use the phone's advertising ID (that permission was removed).

To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and avoid repeating the same ad, Google's systems may receive technical data from the phone, such as the IP address, device model and operating system, language and ad usage data (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped). Google handles that data under its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In some countries (for example, in the European Union) the app shows Google's consent message before requesting ads.

3. Purchases (30-day Pass)

The app offers an optional 30-day pass that removes ads and gives unlimited lives. Payment is processed by Google Play; the app only receives confirmation that the pass is active and its end date, which it stores on the phone. Purchases can be restricted with Google Play parental controls.

4. Internet and permissions

The game works offline. The app uses the internet only for ads and purchases, and checks whether there is a connection (to show “No connection”). It does not ask for camera, microphone, contacts, location or file permissions.

5. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will resolve it.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with its update date.

7. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: game.minipop@gmail.com.
